# campusmate-ai
A multilingual AI college companion for students.
CampusMate AI

Helping Students Learn, Organize & Grow.

CampusMate AI is a smart college companion designed to help students manage their academic life.

Planned Features:

- Notice Intelligence – Understand college notices easily.
- Deadline Tracker – Keep track of important dates.
- Smart To-Do – Organize daily academic tasks.
- Language Buddy – Support for English, Marathi and Hindi.
- Ask CampusMate – Get answers based on college notices.

Goal: To make college life easier, smarter and more organized for students.
