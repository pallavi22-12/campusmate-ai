Project Name: CampusMate AI

Tagline: Helping Students Learn, Organize & Grow.

Problem: College students often find it difficult to understand notices, track deadlines and organize academic tasks.

Solution: CampusMate AI is a multilingual AI college companion that helps students understand notices, manage deadlines and organize their academic life.

Target Users: College students.

Category: Learning & Education

Planned Features:

1. Notice Intelligence
2. Deadline Tracker
3. Smart To-Do
4. English, Marathi and Hindi Language Support
5. Ask CampusMate

Technology: BharatCode AI Model API
